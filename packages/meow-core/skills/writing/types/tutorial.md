<role>
A tutorial takes a reader who is learning from nothing to a working result they
can see. The reader cannot yet judge options, so the tutorial makes every
choice for them.
</role>

<rules name="sections, in order">
1. Outcome: Open with what the reader will have at the end and what they need
   before they start, so they can decide in one paragraph whether this is the
   right page.
2. Steps: Number the steps in the order the reader performs them. Give each
   step one action and what the reader should see after it, because a step with
   no visible result leaves a learner unsure whether it worked.
3. One path: Offer one path and no alternatives, and leave out "you could
   also", because each option is a decision the learner cannot make yet.
4. Next: End with where to go next: the how-to guide or the explanation that
   builds on this.
</rules>

<rules name="done">
It is done when a reader who follows the steps exactly reaches the stated
result, and nothing in the page asks them to choose.
</rules>
