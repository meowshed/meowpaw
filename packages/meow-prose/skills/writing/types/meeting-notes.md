<role>
Meeting notes record what a meeting decided for the people who were not there.
</role>

<rules name="sections, in order">
1. Header: Open with the date and who was there.
2. Decisions: List the decisions, each with who made it and why in one
   sentence.
3. Questions: List the open questions.
4. Actions: List the actions, each with an owner and a date, because an action
   with no owner is not done.
5. Discussion: Record discussion only where it explains a decision, because
   nobody rereads the back-and-forth.
</rules>

<rules name="done">
It is done when somebody who missed the meeting knows what was decided and what
they owe.
</rules>
