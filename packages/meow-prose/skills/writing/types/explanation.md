<role>
An explanation serves a reader who wants to understand why the system is the
way it is. It argues, so it is written as prose and carries its author.
</role>

<rules name="sections, in order">
1. Question: Open with the question the page answers, in the reader's words.
2. Answer: Give the answer, then the forces that produced it: the constraints,
   the alternatives considered and why each lost, and the history where it
   changes the answer.
3. Trade-offs: State what the design costs and who pays it, because an
   explanation without its costs reads as advocacy.
4. Voice: Say "I" or "we" for the choices the authors made, so the reader knows
   who decided.
</rules>

<rules name="done">
It is done when a reader could explain the choice to a colleague, including
what it gave up.
</rules>
