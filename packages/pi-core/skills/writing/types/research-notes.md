<role>
Research notes are material for a decision nobody has made yet, written so
somebody else can argue with them.
</role>

<rules name="sections, in order">
1. Answer: Open with the question and the answer so far in five to eight
   sentences, so a reader who stops there knows where you stand and how sure
   you are.
2. Findings: Give each finding what you checked, where, what you found and what
   it changes, and mark each position as a position, with what would reverse
   it.
3. Unknowns: Give the unmeasured and the unverified a section of their own,
   with the same weight as the measured.
4. Open questions: Close with the open questions, each with an owner or a next
   step, and
   leave out how the notes evolved.
</rules>

<rules name="done">
It is done when a reader can disagree with a specific finding and see what
evidence would settle it.
</rules>
