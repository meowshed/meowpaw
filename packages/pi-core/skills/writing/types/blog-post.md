<role>
An engineering blog post tells one story with one thesis to a reader who chose
to read it, so it has to earn the next paragraph.
</role>

<rules name="sections, in order">
1. Pain: Open with a concrete pain in the first line.
2. Story: Then the problem, the approach, the implementation with code, the
   edge cases, and what to do next.
3. Length: Keep it to one thesis, 600 to 1,500 words and at most three
   second-level headings.
4. Voice: Write "I" for your own story and "we" for a team's.
</rules>

<rules name="done">
It is done when a reader could state the thesis in one sentence and reproduce
the approach.
</rules>
