# REQ-1004

**Withdrawn. Replaced by REQ-3184.**

It read: the mechanically checkable half of the writing standard MUST be a
check.

It divided the standard where the defects do not fall. A check over words
caught three trivial findings in this repository, and the defects that reached
`main` were shape and judgement: bold fragments standing in for headings,
openers announcing how many items follow, sentences past any useful length, and
a borrowed vocabulary nobody noticed. A person reading found all of them.

Writing a check for those means writing a check that guesses at meaning, which
`CLAUDE.md` already rules out: a check tripping on what it should not gets
switched off within a week, and takes the rules beside it with it.
