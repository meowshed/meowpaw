# REQ-3652

Every record in the repository MUST meet the shape of the seven-step chain once its epic lands, with no `## Cover`, `## Verified`, `checked-at` or `## Open review findings` left and no citation of a kept run file.

A record left in the old shape keeps teaching the old chain to whoever reads it, and a check that allows both shapes forever never contracts (the owner's instruction).
