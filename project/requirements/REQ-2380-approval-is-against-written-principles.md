# REQ-2380

An approval the harness makes on its own behalf MUST be made against written
principles - the constitution, the requirements in force and the record that
authorised the work - and MUST NOT be made on preference.

A judgement against a written standard is one a person can check in the
morning; a judgement on taste is one they can only accept or repeat.
