# REQ-1254

A design with a security-relevant boundary MUST state what is protected and
who it is defended against, ordered by likelihood of causing damage.

Ordering by severity spends the design's effort where the damage is imagined
rather than where it comes from; REQ-2784 is what puts the insider first.
