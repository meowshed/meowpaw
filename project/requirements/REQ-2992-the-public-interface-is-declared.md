# REQ-2992

The harness MUST declare what its public interface is, naming the verbs and
their outcomes, the artifact kinds and their front matter, the record's paths
and identifier formats, the profile's keys, the command surface and the
evidence format.

Without the list, every change is either breaking or not depending on who is
asked.
