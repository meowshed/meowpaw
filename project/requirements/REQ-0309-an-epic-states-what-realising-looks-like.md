# REQ-0309

An epic MUST state its acceptance criteria: every requirement its authorising
record addresses closed or deferred with a reason, every task accounted for,
and nothing outstanding that checking the record would report.

These are what verifying the epic is measured against, and they are written
from the authorising decision rather than from the task list, or the epic is
verified against its own plan.
