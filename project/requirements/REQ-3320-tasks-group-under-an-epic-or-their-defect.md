# REQ-3320

**Withdrawn. Replaced by REQ-4002.**

It read: the harness MUST NOT write any grouping of a task other than the epic that realises its authorising record or the defect that carries it, in the record or on a tracker, where a milestone, a project, a label and a parent issue are each a grouping even when it stands for the task's epic or defect, the epic's or the defect's own issue as a parent included.

Withdrawn by ADR-3090.
