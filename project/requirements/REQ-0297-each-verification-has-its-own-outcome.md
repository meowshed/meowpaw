# REQ-0297

**Withdrawn by ADR-2300. Replaced by REQ-3602.**

It read: the outcome of verifying a task MUST be a status on that task, and the outcome of verifying an epic MUST be a status on the epic and, where a requirement its record addresses is closed by nothing, a reported gap.

ADR-2300 closes a requirement with the tasks that name it and keeps no verification step, evidence file or record review, and REQ-3602 states what holds in its place.
