# REQ-1006

**Withdrawn. Replaced by REQ-3186.**

It read: the half of the writing standard that requires judgement MUST be a
review criterion and MUST NOT be made into a check.

It named a half. REQ-1004 named the other one and was withdrawn with it,
because the standard divides nowhere near where that line was drawn: what
looked mechanical was trivia, and what mattered needed a reader. The
replacement states the rule for the whole standard.
