# REQ-3172

An acceptance criterion MUST state something the project itself can bring
about. Where realising a decision depends on behaviour a third party controls,
the criterion MUST state what the project ships, and the dependency MUST be
recorded as a risk rather than written into the criterion.

A criterion that asks a platform to behave is a criterion the project cannot
satisfy by working harder, and an epic held open by one reports nothing about
the work it contains.
