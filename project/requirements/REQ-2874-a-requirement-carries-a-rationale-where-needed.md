# REQ-2874

A requirement MUST carry a rationale where the obligation would otherwise look
arbitrary, and MUST omit one where the text already carries its reason.

A rule without its reason is applied literally and wrongly, which this corpus
found in its own material; and naming a document to read is not a rationale.
