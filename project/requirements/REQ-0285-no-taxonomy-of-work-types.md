# REQ-0285

The harness MUST NOT maintain a taxonomy of work types, and MUST derive the
kind of a piece of work from the record that authorised it.

A maintained taxonomy is a boundary argument that never ends, and every type
anyone proposed for it already routes through a decision or a defect.
