# REQ-3188

The writing standard MUST produce prose that carries its content in the fewest
tokens that content allows, and a text MUST NOT lose content to become shorter.

Every word the harness writes is paid for twice: once when it is produced, and
again on every turn that carries it. What can go is the part carrying nothing,
which is a frame announcing a claim, a sentence restating its neighbour, a
paragraph introducing the next one, and a hedge with no uncertainty under it.
What stays is what REQ-0950 protects: a verb in a verification, a finding in a
review, a question in a list of gaps, a hedge carrying real doubt.

The line between the two is a judgement, so a change to the standard aimed at
length is measured on cost and on quality together, and a saving that lowers
quality is reported as a loss rather than as a win on tokens.
