# REQ-1136

A prompt MUST state what to do and show the wanted form in a positive
example, rather than only naming what to avoid.

A model follows a shown form more reliably than a prohibition, and a
prohibition alone leaves it to guess what to write instead. Where a prompt
shows a failing example, as REQ-1126 requires for a judgement, the corrected
form sits beside it.
