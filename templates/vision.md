# <Project>

## What it is

Two or three paragraphs. What this is, and what it replaces or competes with —
name the alternative, and say why it was not enough. A description with no
alternative is unfalsifiable.

## The problem

The pain, at the moment it happens, for a named person. Not "users struggle with
X" but where they are and what is in their hands. Describe no solution here.

## Who it is for

| Audience | Wants | What they do today instead |
| -------- | ----- | -------------------------- |
|          |       |                            |

The third column is the one that matters. An audience with no alternative is not
an audience.

## What it will not do

Non-goals, stated so they can be cited against a proposal ([R-H-323]). A vision
that never refuses anything settles no argument and is decoration.

## Where it is going

The direction, not a roadmap. What would be true in a year if this worked.

## What we will not trade away

The constraints that survive pressure. This is what a later argument gets
settled by.
