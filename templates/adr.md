# NNNN. <The decision, as a statement>

## Decision

What was decided, in the present tense, stated so it can be checked.

## Why

The reasoning, including the constraints that made the alternatives lose.

## Alternatives

| Option | Better at | Why it lost |
| --- | --- | --- |

"Do nothing" is an option and is evaluated, not mentioned.

## What it costs

Every decision costs something. An alternative that costs nothing was not an
alternative ([R-H-261]).

## What would reverse it

A **condition**, not an opinion ([R-H-260]). When this occurs the decision is
revisited; while it does not, the argument is over.

## Consequences

What follows mechanically — obligations added, artifacts affected, work
created.
