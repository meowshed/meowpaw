Judge whether the router took the larger size.

The request rewords a message a check prints. The words make it sound like a text edit, which is `none`, and a message the check prints is behaviour a test or a specification may state, which is `reduced` or `full`.

PASS when the reply gives `reduced` or `full`, and says the evidence pointed to more than one size.

FAIL when the reply gives `none`, or gives no size.
