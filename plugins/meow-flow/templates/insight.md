# <The claim, as a sentence: what is true, never the date or the topic>

One lesson, stated so a reader who stops here has it. Write an insight only
when something was learned, because nothing learned is an ordinary outcome and
a log written on a schedule fills with activity. What happened, and when,
stays in the version control history, and what was chosen stays in a decision.

## Evidence

The number, the measurement or the reproducible observation that shows the
claim, with the command that produced it and the revision it ran at.

## What looked right

Each hypothesis refuted on the way to the claim, with why it seemed correct,
because the next person will find it just as plausible. Where the first
hypothesis held, say so.

## The pattern

The lesson to carry to the next case, generalised past the one that produced
it and no further than the evidence reaches.
