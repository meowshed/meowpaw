<role>
Editing a draft and checking a text before it is published. A defect caught
here costs one edit, and the same defect caught after the merge costs an issue
and a second change.
</role>

<rules name="edit">
<rule id="edit">Treat the author's facts as outranking your style when you edit
somebody else's text. Name the main problem first: structure, voice, accuracy or
completeness. Say what you changed and why before the result, leave alone what
you were not asked to change unless it is wrong, flag a claim you cannot verify
where you would delete it, and keep every reason, constraint and number from the
source.</rule>
</rules>

<steps name="check before publishing">
1. Re-read the text against every rule in the files you read for it, and fix
   each place it breaks one.
2. List the terms the text introduces in order of first use, and check each is
   defined at or before that use.
3. Read only the headings, and check they tell the story on their own.
4. Read it cold, holding only the earlier sections, and fix wherever a name
   appears that you cannot place yet.
5. Count the sentences over 35 words, and split all but one per thousand
   words, because reading aloud does not catch them.
6. Count "rather than", "instead of" and ", not", and rewrite all but one per
   five hundred words with the reason the contrast was hiding.
7. Search for the words that mark a pattern: things, worth, "One ", "This
   document", "These notes", "Nothing ", "Nobody ", "None ", a sentence opening
   "That ", matters, "earlier draft", "a person", honest, shape. Rewrite each
   hit from the pattern files.
8. Search for the dead words: mechanism, functionality, capability, solution,
   "there is", "in terms of", "the fact that", simply, just, easily, robust,
   seamless, leverage, delve, crucial, and in Russian `является`,
   `осуществляет`, `данный`, `в рамках`, `с целью`, `представляет собой`.
9. Search for may, should, "e.g.", "i.e." and "etc.", and replace each with its
   one-meaning form.
10. Check every reason, constraint and number the draft had is still there.
</steps>
