<role>
A design proposal asks for a decision that has not been made. Its reader is
evaluating, so the first screen has to carry the whole case.
</role>

<rules name="sections, in order">
<rule id="summary">Open with five sentences that stand alone: the problem, the
proposal, its cost, its risk, and the ask.</rule>
<rule id="scope">State the goals and the non-goals, because a non-goal written
down ends an argument before it starts.</rule>
<rule id="design">Describe the design, then the alternatives with why each
lost.</rule>
<rule id="risks">Give each risk its impact and its mitigation, and each
mitigation its cost and how anyone will tell it worked.</rule>
<rule id="rollout">Describe the rollout and how it is undone.</rule>
<rule id="unknowns">State what is unmeasured as plainly as what is measured,
and close with the open questions, each with an owner.</rule>
</rules>

<rules name="done">
<rule id="done">It is done when a reader who stops after the summary knows what
is asked and what it costs, and one who reads on can argue with every
claim.</rule>
</rules>
