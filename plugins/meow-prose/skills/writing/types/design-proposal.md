<role>
A design proposal asks for a decision that has not been made. Its reader is
evaluating, so the first screen has to carry the whole case.
</role>

<rules name="sections, in order">
1. Summary: Open with five sentences that stand alone: the problem, the
   proposal, its cost, its risk, and the ask.
2. Scope: State the goals and the non-goals, because a non-goal written down
   ends an argument before it starts.
3. Design: Describe the design, then the alternatives with why each lost.
4. Risks: Give each risk its impact and its mitigation, and each mitigation its
   cost and how anyone will tell it worked.
5. Rollout: Describe the rollout and how it is undone.
6. Unknowns: State what is unmeasured as plainly as what is measured, and close
   with the open questions, each with an owner.
</rules>

<rules name="done">
It is done when a reader who stops after the summary knows what is asked and
what it costs, and one who reads on can argue with every claim.
</rules>
