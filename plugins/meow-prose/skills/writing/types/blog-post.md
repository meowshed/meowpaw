<role>
An engineering blog post tells one story with one thesis to a reader who chose
to read it, so it has to earn the next paragraph.
</role>

<rules name="sections, in order">
<rule id="pain">Open with a concrete pain in the first line.</rule>
<rule id="story">Then the problem, the approach, the implementation with code,
the edge cases, and what to do next.</rule>
<rule id="length">Keep it to one thesis, 600 to 1,500 words and at most three
second-level headings.</rule>
<rule id="voice">Write "I" for your own story and "we" for a team's.</rule>
</rules>

<rules name="done">
<rule id="done">It is done when a reader could state the thesis in one sentence
and reproduce the approach.</rule>
</rules>
