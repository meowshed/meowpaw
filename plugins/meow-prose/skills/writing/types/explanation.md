<role>
An explanation serves a reader who wants to understand why the system is the
way it is. It argues, so it is written as prose and carries its author.
</role>

<rules name="sections, in order">
<rule id="question">Open with the question the page answers, in the reader's
words.</rule>
<rule id="answer">Give the answer, then the forces that produced it: the
constraints, the alternatives considered and why each lost, and the history
where it changes the answer.</rule>
<rule id="trade-offs">State what the design costs and who pays it, because an
explanation without its costs reads as advocacy.</rule>
<rule id="voice">Say "I" or "we" for the choices the authors made, so the
reader knows who decided.</rule>
</rules>

<rules name="done">
<rule id="done">It is done when a reader could explain the choice to a
colleague, including what it gave up.</rule>
</rules>
