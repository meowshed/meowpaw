<role>
A decision record says what was chosen, what lost, and how anyone will know it
worked. Its reader is evaluating, so the decision comes first.
</role>

<rules name="sections, in order">
<rule id="decision">The decision, stated so it can be checked, naming the
requirements it addresses.</rule>
<rule id="why">Why, including the constraints that made the alternatives
lose.</rule>
<rule id="alternatives">The alternatives, doing nothing among them, each with
what it was better at and why it lost.</rule>
<rule id="cost">What it costs, and who pays.</rule>
<rule id="reversal">What would reverse it, as something observable.</rule>
<rule id="consequences">The consequences, each with a concrete effect.</rule>
<rule id="realised">How you will know it was realised, written now so whoever
verifies it later does not invent the test.</rule>
<rule id="unsettled">What it does not settle, so nobody assumes it did.</rule>
</rules>

<rules name="done">
<rule id="done">It is done when a verifier could test every claim in it without
asking the author.</rule>
</rules>
