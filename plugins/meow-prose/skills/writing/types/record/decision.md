<role>
A decision record says what was chosen, what lost, and how anyone will know it
worked. Its reader is evaluating, so the decision comes first.
</role>

<rules name="sections, in order">
1. Decision: The decision, stated so it can be checked, naming the requirements
   it addresses.
2. Why: Why, including the constraints that made the alternatives lose.
3. Alternatives: The alternatives, doing nothing among them, each with what it
   was better at and why it lost.
4. Cost: What it costs, and who pays.
5. Reversal: What would reverse it, as something observable.
6. Consequences: The consequences, each with a concrete effect.
7. How you will know: How you will know it was realised, written now so whoever
   verifies
   it later does not invent the test.
8. What it does not settle: What it does not settle, so nobody assumes it did.
</rules>

<rules name="done">
It is done when a verifier could test every claim in it without asking the
author.
</rules>
