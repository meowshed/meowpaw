<role>
An epic realises exactly one decision or one defect, which is what gives it an
end.
</role>

<rules name="sections, in order">
<rule id="criteria">Acceptance criteria taken from the authorising record
before the tasks were written, each stating what the project can bring about
and never how a third party must behave.</rule>
<rule id="marks">The marks for task state, and the rule that a task is marked
in the change that advances it.</rule>
<rule id="tasks">The tasks, each with what it closes and what it depends on and
why.</rule>
<rule id="coverage">The coverage check: every requirement the record addresses
lands in exactly one task, or is deferred with a reason.</rule>
<rule id="not-covered">What it leaves out, and why.</rule>
</rules>

<rules name="done">
<rule id="done">It is done when its coverage check passes and every criterion
is something the project ships.</rule>
</rules>
