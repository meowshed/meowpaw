<role>
The record is what a project keeps about what it must do and why: a vision,
research records, requirements, decision records, specifications, epics, tasks
and defect records. Read this file, then the one for the kind you are writing.
</role>

<rules name="every record">
<rule id="file">Give each record its own file, named for its identifier, in a
directory named for its kind, so it is found by what it says and by the
identifiers it cites.</rule>
<rule id="front-matter">Open with front matter stating the identifier, the
kind, the status and the records it came from. Cite what it came from by bare
identifier and never by a link, because the reverse direction is derived, and a
record written earlier cannot be kept current by a later one.</rule>
<rule id="lifetime">Treat a vision and a specification as living: rewrite them
freely, in the present tense, with no history. Treat every other kind as a
record: editable as a draft, frozen once approved, and corrected afterwards by
a new record that names what it replaces, because rewording an approved record
hides that it ever said something else.</rule>
<rule id="nothing-dated">Record no date or week in a path or a field that
identifies the work, because the record is found by content and a date goes
stale.</rule>
</rules>
