<role>
A research record says what was read and what it showed, so a requirement can
cite evidence instead of an argument.
</role>

<rules name="sections, in order">
1. Summary: A summary answering the question in five to eight sentences.
2. Question: The question, and why it needed answering.
3. Method: The method: what was read or run, when, and the limits of that.
4. Findings: The findings, each naming the source it comes from, read at the
   primary source and not a summary of it.
5. Conclusions: Numbered conclusions, each one a requirement could cite.
6. Sources: The sources, each with what it supported.
</rules>

<rules name="done">
It is done when every conclusion traces to a finding and every finding to a
source.
</rules>
