<role>
A vision says what the project is and what it will not become. Every later
decision is checked against it, so it is short and it states limits.
</role>

<rules name="sections, in order">
1. What it is: What the project is, in one paragraph a newcomer understands.
2. The problem: The problem it answers, and for whom.
3. Non-goals: What it will not do, because a stated non-goal ends arguments
   before
   they start.
4. Direction: Where it is going, and what it will not trade away to get there.
</rules>

<rules name="done">
It is done when a newcomer could reject a proposal on the vision alone.
</rules>
