<role>
A vision says what the project is and what it will not become. Every later
decision is checked against it, so it is short and it states limits.
</role>

<rules name="sections, in order">
<rule id="what">What the project is, in one paragraph a newcomer
understands.</rule>
<rule id="problem">The problem it answers, and for whom.</rule>
<rule id="not">What it will not do, because a stated non-goal ends arguments
before they start.</rule>
<rule id="direction">Where it is going, and what it will not trade away to get
there.</rule>
</rules>

<rules name="done">
<rule id="done">It is done when a newcomer could reject a proposal on the
vision alone.</rule>
</rules>
