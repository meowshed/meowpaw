<role>
A tutorial takes a reader who is learning from nothing to a working result they
can see. The reader cannot yet judge options, so the tutorial makes every
choice for them.
</role>

<rules name="sections, in order">
<rule id="outcome">Open with what the reader will have at the end and what they
need before they start, so they can decide in one paragraph whether this is the
right page.</rule>
<rule id="steps">Number the steps in the order the reader performs them. Give
each step one action and what the reader should see after it, because a step
with no visible result leaves a learner unsure whether it worked.</rule>
<rule id="one-path">Offer one path and no alternatives, and leave out "you
could also", because each option is a decision the learner cannot make
yet.</rule>
<rule id="next">End with where to go next: the how-to guide or the explanation
that builds on this.</rule>
</rules>

<rules name="done">
<rule id="done">It is done when a reader who follows the steps exactly reaches
the stated result, and nothing in the page asks them to choose.</rule>
</rules>
