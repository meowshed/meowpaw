<role>
Research notes are material for a decision nobody has made yet, written so
somebody else can argue with them.
</role>

<rules name="sections, in order">
<rule id="answer">Open with the question and the answer so far in five to eight
sentences, so a reader who stops there knows where you stand and how sure you
are.</rule>
<rule id="findings">Give each finding what you checked, where, what you found
and what it changes, and mark each position as a position, with what would
reverse it.</rule>
<rule id="unknowns">Give the unmeasured and the unverified a section of their
own, with the same weight as the measured.</rule>
<rule id="open">Close with the open questions, each with an owner or a next
step, and leave out how the notes evolved.</rule>
</rules>

<rules name="done">
<rule id="done">It is done when a reader can disagree with a specific finding
and see what evidence would settle it.</rule>
</rules>
