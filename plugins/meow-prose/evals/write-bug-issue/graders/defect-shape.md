Judge the issue.

PASS when it opens with what is broken, then says what was expected, what happened and the smallest steps that reproduce it, as sentences or a numbered list of steps, and not under bold labels such as "**Expected**".

FAIL when it opens with background, leaves out the expected behaviour or the steps, or sets the parts under bold labels.
